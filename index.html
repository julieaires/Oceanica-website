TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Maritime and coastal engineering services

Comprehensive multidisciplinary engineering across six core marine disciplines, supporting feasibility studies, detailed design, permitting, and construction supervision.

Port and Harbor Facilities

High-capacity berthing, cargo handling, and navigation infrastructure

We deliver full-lifecycle engineering for commercial ports, industrial jetties, and multipurpose terminals—optimizing berth layout, vessel manoeuvring, structural durability, and capital dredging volumes.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • Gravity, diaphragm, sheet-pile, and piled quay wall structures
  • Container, bulk, liquid, and Ro-Ro terminal layouts & crane rails
  • Berthing energy, mooring analysis, and fendering system design
  • Navigation channel geometry, turning basins, and dredging plans

All structural and navigational designs comply with PIANC Working Group guidelines, Eurocodes, and British Standards for maritime works.

Breakwaters and Coastal Protection Works

Wave attenuation, shoreline stabilization, and climate-resilient defenses

We engineer rubble-mound and composite breakwaters, revetments, and beach stabilization schemes backed by spectral wave transformation, overtopping analysis, and physical flume testing oversight.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • Rubble-mound, concrete armour unit, and vertical caisson breakwaters
  • 2D/3D wave agitation, harbour resonance, and overtopping modelling
  • Groynes, seawalls, revetments, and sediment transport assessments
  • Nature-based coastal resilience and sea-level rise adaptation

Designs are verified in accordance with the CIRIA/CUR Rock Manual, EurOtop overtopping manual, and site-specific extreme metocean return periods.

Marine Pipelines and Outfalls

Subsea intake systems, brine diffusers, and municipal wastewater outfalls

From route selection through the surf zone to multi-port diffuser hydraulics, we design HDPE, steel, and GRP marine pipelines engineered for hydrodynamic stability and marine water-quality compliance.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • Desalination seawater intakes and high-salinity brine outfall diffusers
  • Near-field and far-field plume dispersion modelling (CORMIX / MIKE)
  • On-bottom stability, concrete ballast collar, and trenching design
  • Float-and-sink marine installation engineering and shore-crossing works

Engineered to meet DNV-RP-F109 stability standards, EU Water Framework Directive mixing-zone criteria, and IFC environmental guidelines.

Offshore and Underwater Structures

Fixed subsea foundations, offshore mooring systems, and marine energy support

We provide structural, hydrodynamic, and corrosion engineering for offshore platforms, single-point moorings, subsea templates, and marine cable protection systems.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • Piled jackets, monopiles, gravity-base, and subsea foundation structures
  • CALM buoys, multi-buoy moorings, and offshore loading dolphins
  • Cathodic protection (sacrificial anode & ICCP) and coating specifications
  • Subsea cable landfall, mattressing, and scour protection engineering

Executed in alignment with ISO 19902, DNV offshore standards, and API RP 2A structural and fatigue design codes.

Waterfront Developments and Marine Terminals

Marinas, cruise piers, coastal reclamation, and integrated waterfront masterplans

We bridge coastal engineering and civil infrastructure to deliver sheltered marinas, passenger terminals, land reclamation edges, and waterfront public realm works.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • Yacht marinas, floating pontoon systems, and superyacht berths
  • Cruise and ferry terminals, passenger gangways, and promenades
  • Land reclamation bunds, ground improvement, and edge retention walls
  • Marine utilities, shoreline flushing studies, and environmental permitting

Integrated hydrodynamic water-exchange modelling ensures basin water quality alongside safe berthing tranquility.

Geotechnical and Geophysical Surveys and Studies

Marine site investigation scoping, seabed characterization, and foundation design

We plan, supervise, and interpret hydrographic, geophysical, and offshore geotechnical campaigns to de-risk marine foundations, dredging, and trenching operations.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • Technical specifications & QA/QC supervision for marine site campaigns
  • Multibeam bathymetry, side-scan sonar, sub-bottom profiling & magnetometer
  • Offshore borehole, CPTu, and laboratory testing interpretation
  • Seismic liquefaction, slope stability, pile drivability, and dredgeability

Deliverables follow IHO S-44 hydrographic standards, ISO 19901-8 marine soil investigations, and Eurocode 7 geotechnical design requirements.
