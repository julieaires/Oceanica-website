OUR SERVICES

Maritime and coastal engineering services

End-to-end engineering for marine and coastal infrastructure, from survey to supervision. Explore our six core disciplines below.

DISCIPLINE 01

Port and Harbor Facilities

Integrated planning, berthing structures and basin hydrodynamics for resilient maritime terminals.

We deliver comprehensive engineering for greenfield port developments, terminal upgrades and navigational infrastructure, balancing vessel operational safety with long-term structural durability.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • • Layout and navigational access design
  • • Quays, piers, dolphins and jetties
  • • Ro-Ro and container terminals, mooring & fendering
  • • Port basin hydraulics, dredging studies and manoeuvring simulations

All structural and navigational designs adhere strictly to PIANC guidelines, British Standards (BS) and Eurocode frameworks, supporting clients seamlessly from master planning through tender documentation and site supervision.

DISCIPLINE 02

Breakwaters and Coastal Protection Works

Wave attenuation, shoreline stabilization and climate-resilient coastal defense structures.

Built on rigorous site-specific metocean and wave-climate analysis, our coastal engineering solutions protect harbors, shorelines and waterfront assets against extreme storm events and long-term sea-level rise.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • • Rubble mound and caisson breakwaters
  • • 2D/3D numerical and physical hydraulic modelling
  • • Revetments, groynes and erosion control for beaches, dunes and cliffs
  • • Nature-based solutions including beach nourishment and dune restoration

Every coastal defense scheme is evaluated for sediment transport balance, overtopping performance and environmental compliance under EU Water Framework Directive (WFD) and international coastal engineering standards.

DISCIPLINE 03

Marine Pipelines and Outfalls

Subsea routing, plume dispersion hydraulics and hydrodynamic stability for intakes and outfalls.

We engineer submarine pipelines for desalination plants, municipal wastewater treatment facilities and coastal industrial intakes, integrating seabed morphology with strict dilution and water-quality requirements.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • • Desalination brine discharge and treated wastewater outfalls
  • • On-bottom and buried hydrodynamic stability & trenching analysis
  • • Multi-port diffuser hydraulic design and near-field dispersion modelling
  • • Shore-crossing protection, concrete ballast weights and onshore interface detailing

Designs integrate environmental permitting criteria under EU WFD and IFC Performance Standards, ensuring constructable marine installation sequences from float-and-sink operations to post-lay protection.

DISCIPLINE 04

Offshore and Underwater Structures

Structural and geotechnical engineering for fixed and floating marine assets.

We provide specialized structural analysis, foundation design and marine installation consulting for offshore platforms, subsea infrastructure and nearshore floating systems operating in demanding metocean environments.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • • Subsea foundations, driven monopiles and jacket structures
  • • Catenary and taut mooring systems for floating platforms
  • • Cathodic protection systems and marine corrosion control
  • • Installation engineering, lifting analysis and offshore constructability

Structural verification encompasses fatigue life assessment, extreme wave-load combinations and seabed scour protection in accordance with Eurocode, DNV and API offshore engineering standards.

DISCIPLINE 05

Waterfront Developments and Marine Terminals

Master planning and marine infrastructure for marinas, cruise ports and coastal destinations.

We bridge marine civil engineering with architectural and operational master planning to create functional, accessible and environmentally integrated marinas, passenger terminals and coastal promenades.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • • Master planning and berth-mix optimization for marinas and cruise ports
  • • Quay walls, floating pontoon docks, gangways and passenger terminal facilities
  • • Marine utilities integration (shore power, bunkering, water & firefighting)
  • • Regulatory permitting dossiers and Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA)

From initial feasibility and basin tranquillity studies to full permitting approval and detailed construction drawings, we ensure seamless coordination across marine, landside and regulatory stakeholders.

DISCIPLINE 06

Geotechnical and Geophysical Surveys and Studies

Marine site characterization, seabed mapping and seismic ground engineering.

Reliable marine structures start beneath the seabed. We specify, supervise and interpret nearshore and offshore site investigations to de-risk foundation design, dredging and subsea trenching.

Key engineering scopes & deliverables:

  • • Marine survey campaign design, technical specifications and tender supervision
  • • Multibeam bathymetry, side-scan sonar and sub-bottom seismic profiling interpretation
  • • CPT, SPT, deep borehole and vibrocore laboratory & parameter synthesis
  • • Soil liquefaction potential, slope stability and seismic site-response assessment

Ground models and geotechnical design reports are prepared in full compliance with Eurocode 7 and Eurocode 8 seismic provisions, translating complex subsurface data into actionable foundation parameters.
